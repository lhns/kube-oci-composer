kube-oci-builder is installed.

This component runs Dockerfile builds as Jobs. Read what that costs before relying on it:

  https://github.com/lhns/kube-oci-composer/blob/main/docs/adr/0025-dockerfile-builds-as-a-second-kind.md

In short, DockerBuild makes a WEAKER promise than ImageComposition:

  * its output digest is an observation recorded in status, not a function of its spec
  * two builds of the same commit may differ, so spec-hash tags are first-writer-wins
  * storage stops being disposable: a rebuild after loss may not reproduce the digest a tag names
  * provenance is scanned rather than exact

If what you need is "take a released artifact and put it in an image" — including one your CI
already published as an image — use ImageComposition. Since ADR 0024 it can take files out of an
image directly.
